HeavyPro does not use vJoy, FSRealistic, or ChasePlane.

It subscribes to AXIS_ELEVATOR_SET, AXIS_AILERONS_SET and AXIS_RUDDER_SET at the highest maskable SimConnect priority, shapes the value from aircraft weight, and sends that value on. The physical stick stays bound in MSFS.

This cannot change lift or Fenix flight-by-wire if that aircraft reads the controller itself and ignores SimConnect axis events.
